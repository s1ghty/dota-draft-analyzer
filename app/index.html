How this works

Role (1–5)

  • Sets which position you're drafting for.
  • Changes recommendations two ways: which enemy heroes count as the biggest threat (a support-killing ganker matters more at pos 4/5, a lane bully matters more at pos 1/2/3), and which candidates even show up (a hero almost never played at your role is excluded, not just ranked low).
  • Change it any time — everything re-scores instantly.

Your Team / Enemy Team / Ban

  • Add to picks which list a click adds to. Click a mode, then click heroes in the grid.
  • Your Team / Enemy Team: up to 5 each.
  • Ban: removes a hero from consideration entirely (no side) — for picks that are gone, not just disliked.
  • Reset clears all three at once.

Search

  • Type in the search box, or just start typing anywhere (like the in-game hero picker) — no need to click into the box first.
  • Matches nicknames and aliases too, not just the full hero name.
  • Already-picked/banned heroes always dim while searching, even if they match, since you can't add them again.
  • Esc clears the search.

Ranked Candidates

  • The bar length is relative to this list only — not a win-rate or a percentage. It combines: counter matchup vs. enemies, synergy with your team, role fit at your chosen role, and baseline strength, minus how hard the enemy already counters the pick.
  • The one-line reason under each name calls out its biggest single contributor (e.g. a strong matchup or synergy).
  • #1
    Puck
    +9% vs their Invoker, solid all-round pick for this role
    vs Invoker: Illusory Orb and Waning Rift win the lane outright, and Phase Shift dodges his combos in a fight.

Items to buy (Biggest Threat panel)

  • These items are for you to buy, to deal with that enemy threat — never what the enemy is buying.

Items to buy against this threat:

(The separate Suggested Build panel is different — that's the full item build for your own picked hero, not a counter list.)

Counter note text

  • The short quoted line under a candidate or threat (like the Puck/Invoker example above) is a curated, wiki-sourced note about that specific hero-vs-hero matchup — extra context, not part of the score.
  • Under a candidate, it only ever explains why that candidate counters the enemy — never the reverse.
